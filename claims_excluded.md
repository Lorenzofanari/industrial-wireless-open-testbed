# Claims excluded

This artifact explicitly does **not** make any of the following claims. They are
out of scope by design.

- **Real radio performance.** Synthetic demo-mode outputs are not real radio
  measurements.
- **Radio-standard validation.** No validation or conformance for Wi-Fi, BLE,
  IEEE 802.15.4/Zigbee, LoRa, or SDR standards; profiles are technology-like.
- **Offensive cybersecurity.** No jamming, flooding, deauthentication,
  exploitation, or unauthorised scanning functionality.
- **Interference mitigation.** No interference detection, mitigation, or
  protection of any kind.
- **Wireless scheduling.** No scheduling algorithm is implemented or evaluated.
- **Industrial certification.** No safety, SIL, PROFIsafe, or certification
  claim.
- **Deployment-ready instrumentation.** This is a teaching/research artifact, not
  a production-ready or deployment-ready system.

See [`claims_included.md`](claims_included.md) for what the artifact does claim,
and [`docs/limitations_and_non_goals.md`](docs/limitations_and_non_goals.md) for
the full scope statement.
