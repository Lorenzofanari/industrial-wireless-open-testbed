# Supplementary materials checklist (MDPI *Hardware*)

Every item below should be uploaded or archived as Supplementary Material for the
paper. The git-ignored `results/` outputs are not shipped; reviewers regenerate
them from `configs/` and `data/` (see "Required for rebuild?").

## Notes

- "Required for rebuild?" marks the minimal set needed to regenerate all
  synthetic demo-mode outputs from a clean clone.
- The `results/` directory is intentionally not shipped: it is regenerated by
  `python scripts/run_demo.py --all`.
- Do not include the local virtual environment (`.venv/`) or caches.
