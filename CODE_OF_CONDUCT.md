# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as
contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
- gracefully accepting constructive criticism;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the science.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- encouraging or facilitating misuse of the artifact contrary to
  [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and [`docs/safety_and_ethics.md`](docs/safety_and_ethics.md);
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the project maintainers (replace with the project's preferred
contact before publication). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the
circumstances.
